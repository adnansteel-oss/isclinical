Why the Regenerating Cleanser from Tata Harper isn't just another face wash

Most cleansers pick one lane — a gentle gel, a harsh scrub, or an acid wash — and stop there. The Regenerating Cleanser stacks three exfoliation methods into a single natural-origin formula, then backs the claims with an actual clinical self-assessment rather than marketing copy alone.

Triple-action formula

Physical, chemical and enzymatic exfoliation, at the same time

Instead of relying on one exfoliation method, this cleanser layers three: apricot microspheres for gentle physical polishing, white willow bark as a natural source of salicylic acid for chemical exfoliation, and pomegranate enzymes to help buff away dulling surface cells. The result is a single daily step that's doing the work of several separate products.

Clinically tested

Results measured, not just promised

In a self-assessment clinical study of 32 women, 93% agreed their skin looked more radiant after a single use, and 90% agreed their skin looked healthier, didn't feel stripped, and felt fresh after four weeks of daily use. That's a meaningfully different claim than "brightens skin" printed on a box with nothing behind it.

Natural-origin, certified

Clean ingredients backed by third-party certification, not a marketing label

The formula is 100% natural origin and 82% organic, holding COSMOS Natural certification through Ecocert Greenlife. It's also certified vegan by the American Vegetarian Association and certified cruelty-free by PETA — certifications that come from outside labs, not the brand's own copywriting.

How to use it

Tata's tip: reach for it dry-faced, first thing in the morning

The brand's own suggestion is to apply the Regenerating Cleanser directly onto dry skin as the first step of your morning routine — the friction against dry skin lets the physical exfoliants do more of their job, while an uplifting grapefruit scent doubles as a wake-up call before the rest of your routine.
