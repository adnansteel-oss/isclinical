How to use iS Clinical Cleansing Complex for best results

Getting results from this cleanser depends on frequency, not just formula. Most dermatologists recommend using it once a day at night, letting the salicylic acid work while you sleep, and switching to a gentler cleanser in the morning if your skin leans dry or sensitive. Twice-daily use works fine for oily or thick skin, but that's where the drying complaints usually start.

Application matters too. A dime-sized amount goes further than you'd think, since the gel foams up quickly once it hits water. Massage it into damp skin for 30 to 60 seconds, focusing on congested areas like the nose, chin, and forehead, then rinse with lukewarm water. Hot water strips the barrier faster than the cleanser ever will.

Consistency, not quantity, is what turns this cleanser into a routine staple instead of a one-time impulse buy.

Here's a simple routine to follow for the first month:

  • Week 1-2: Use once nightly to let skin adjust to the salicylic acid
  • Week 3-4: Add a morning wash if skin tolerates it without tightness
  • Ongoing: Follow immediately with a hydrating serum or a moisturizing complex since the formula doesn't leave residue behind

Post-procedure patients should stick to once daily, and only after their provider clears them to resume actives. Skipping toner right after cleansing and going straight to hydration, ideally with a cooling hydration serum, keeps irritation minimal while the skin barrier resets.

Cleansing Complex vs. Cleansing Complex Polish: which to choose

Confusion between these two products is common, and it's a fair mix-up since they share a name and a base formula. iS Clinical Cleansing Complex Polish adds micro-exfoliating beads to the same salicylic acid and botanical blend, giving you physical exfoliation alongside the chemical kind. That combination makes the Polish version more intense, so it's built for occasional use rather than a daily wash.

Deciding between them comes down to your skin's tolerance for exfoliation. If your skin already handles the standard gel well and craves a deeper reset once or twice a week, the Polish version fits naturally into your routine. Sensitive or reactive skin types usually do better sticking with the original, since the added texture can aggravate compromised barriers, especially post-procedure.

Save the Polish version for a weekly deep-clean, not a daily habit, or you risk over-exfoliating skin that's already working hard.

Here's how the two stack up side by side:

Ultimately, most routines benefit from having both on hand, using the standard formula daily and the Polish as a weekly boost.
