Suppleness isn't just about how much water is in the skin — it's about how well that water is held there. Cold air, indoor heating, and dry climates all pull moisture out faster than skin can replace it, weakening the outer barrier in the process. Once that barrier is compromised, skin starts to look tired even when nothing else about your routine has changed.
