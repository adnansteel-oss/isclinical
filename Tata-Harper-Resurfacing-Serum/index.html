What it actually does

Most exfoliating serums ask you to choose between a gentle formula and one that gets real results. The Tata Harper Resurfacing Serum is built to close that gap. Its AHA and BHA complex works on the skin's surface to lift away the buildup that causes roughness and dullness, while a base of essential fatty acids keeps the moisture barrier intact through the process — so texture improves without the tightness or flaking that harsher exfoliants tend to leave behind.

What sets the formula apart is where its actives come from. Rather than synthetic acids, the serum leans on a Superfruit AHA blend for exfoliation, brightening Kakadu Plum for clarity, and Tomato Carotenoids to even out tone over time. Used daily, the goal isn't a one-time peel effect — it's a slow, steady renewal that leaves pores refined, texture smoother, and skin visibly more luminous with continued use.

It's formulated for all skin types, which is part of why it's become a staple step for people rebuilding a routine around uneven texture or tired-looking skin rather than a specific complaint like acne or deep wrinkles. Think of it less as a spot treatment and more as a daily reset button for your skin's surface.
